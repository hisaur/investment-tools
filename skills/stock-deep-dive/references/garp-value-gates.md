# Value & GARP Investing Quality Gates

This reference defines the quality filters, moat durability criteria, and value trap rejection checklists.

---

## 1. The Buffett / Munger / Fisher Quality Gates

Before calculating DCF or multiples, the business must pass qualitative durability gates:

### Gate 1: Circle of Competence & Business Simplicity
- Can the company's unit economics and primary cash-generation engine be explained in plain English?
- Are long-term demand drivers predictable over a 5-to-10 year horizon?
- If unpredictable or overly reliant on opaque financial engineering, assign to the **"Too Hard Pile"** and pass.

### Gate 2: Economic Moat Durability
Evaluate against the five classical competitive moats:
1. **Network Effects**: Value increases quadratically with every incremental user.
2. **Switching Costs**: High friction, data gravity, or operational risk prevents customer defection.
3. **Cost Advantage**: Scale economies, proprietary low-cost process, or structural logistics edge.
4. **Intangible Assets**: Strong brand pricing power, patents, or exclusive regulatory licenses.
5. **Efficient Scale**: Natural local monopoly or oligopoly in a niche market where new entrants cannot earn cost of capital.

**Moat Test**:
- Is Return on Invested Capital (ROIC) consistently higher than the estimated Cost of Capital (~8-10%) over the last 5 years?
- Has Gross Margin expanded or remained steady in inflationary periods (Pricing Power)?

### Gate 3: Management & Capital Allocation Discipline
- **Owner-Orientation**: Transparent disclosures, reasonable executive compensation tied to ROIC/FCF per share (not vanity revenue targets).
- **Share Buybacks**: Are buybacks executed when the stock is undervalued, or are they mindless repurchases at market peaks to offset option dilution?
- **Reinvestment Efficiency**: Does incremental capital reinvested generate high incremental earnings (Incremental ROIC)?

## 2. 10-Point Value Trap Diagnostic Checklist

Evaluate each item and flag accordingly:
- ✅ **Clear**: No indication of impairment
- ⚠️ **Warning**: Moderate concern requiring close monitoring
- ❌ **Triggered**: Serious structural issue

| # | Red Flag Indicator | Description | Trigger Threshold |
| :-: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Secular / Technological Decline** | Industry is experiencing structural shrinkage (e.g. legacy media, obsolete hardware). | 3-yr Revenue CAGR < -3% without industry cyclicality |
| **2** | **Excessive Leverage & Debt Wall** | Debt load limits reinvestment and creates insolvency risk during downturns. | Net Debt / EBITDA > 3.5x or Altman Z < 1.8 |
| **3** | **Chronic Share Dilution** | Management issues large equity grants that dilute per-share earnings power. | Diluted shares outstanding increasing > 2.0% annually |
| **4** | **Unfunded CapEx / Fake FCF** | Maintenance CapEx is understated; reported FCF does not reflect real cash needs. | FCF consistently < 50% of reported Net Income |
| **5** | **Customer / Supplier Concentration** | A single customer or vendor represents a disproportionate share of business. | Top customer > 20% of total revenue |
| **6** | **Unsupportable Dividend** | High dividend yield is a lure; payout ratio exceeds operating cash generation. | Dividend Payout / FCF > 90% |
| **7** | **Piotroski Deterioration** | Weakening balance sheet, margin compression, or deteriorating asset turnover. | Piotroski F-Score <= 4 / 9 |
| **8** | **Aggressive Revenue Recognition** | Divergence between revenue growth and accounts receivable (stuffing the channel). | A/R growth significantly outpacing sales growth |
| **9** | **Destructive M&A Track Record** | Frequent goodwill write-downs or empire-building acquisitions destroying ROIC. | Goodwill > 40% of assets or repeated write-offs |
| **10** | **Insider Selling Waves** | Executives and directors aggressively offloading shares into the open market. | Zero insider buying + persistent heavy net insider sales |

> **Rule of Thumb**: If **2 or more red flags are ❌ Triggered**, the stock is classified as a likely **Value Trap**. The analyst must pass or assign a heavy risk discount (>35% MoS).
